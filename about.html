About Me

Hi! Welcome to my website. I’m Ashley Mae O. Bernardez, a proud Igorot from Sabangan and Bauko, Mountain Province. I’m a Criminology student, the second child in my family, and a proud dog lover.

I believe in staying strong, protecting my peace, and choosing myself with self-respect.
